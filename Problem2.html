Mehfil

Mehfil is a popular, budget-friendly restaurant chain in Hyderabad (founded 2006) renowned for authentic Hyderabadi Dum Biryani, kebabs, and Mughlai cuisine. Operating with multiple locations, including Hitech City and Madhapur, it offers dine-in, takeaway, and midnight dining options, catering to families and late-night cravings.
